Agentic AI

Agentic AI refers to autonomous software systems powered by large language models that plan, use tools, and execute multi-step workflows with minimal human intervention. According to Daily.dev, "AI agents are intelligent systems capable of autonomously performing tasks such as obtaining information, designing workflows, making decisions, performing actions, and learning."

Autonomy

Operates independently over time to achieve a specific goal rather than just answering a single prompt.

The Agent Loop

Follows a continuous sequence of "plan, act, observe, adapt." (Analytics Vidhya)

Tool Use

Interacts with external software, APIs, databases, and code execution environments.

Reasoning and Planning

Decomposes large goals into smaller, ordered subtasks.

Memory

Utilizes short-term and long-term storage to keep track of prior steps and context.

Feedback and Reflection

Evaluates tool outputs and corrects errors on the fly.

Orchestration

Scales from single-agent setups to multi-agent frameworks where specialized agents collaborate.
